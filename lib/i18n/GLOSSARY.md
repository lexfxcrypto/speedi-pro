# Thai glossary — Speedi Pro

One word per idea, everywhere. A native Thai speaker should review the
whole app before it is promoted; this list is what they review first.

## Never translated
Speedi, Speedi Pro, Yori, LINE, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok,
Google, Apple, App Store. Numbers stay as Arabic numerals.

## Tone
- Friendly app Thai, short. Address the user as คุณ where a pronoun is
  needed; usually leave it out, as Thai UI does.
- No gendered particles (ครับ / ค่ะ): the app does not know who is reading.
- Translate the meaning, not the words. "Tap to call" → แตะเพื่อโทร.
- Keep emoji, punctuation marks and {placeholders} exactly as in English.
